## Hi there 👋

Welcome to my digital hoarding space 🐹

This blog is essentially my attempt at becoming a *productive* hamster. You know how hamsters stuff everything into their cheeks for later? Well, I stuff knowledge, notes, and random thoughts into this blog. The difference is that I actually plan to come back and use them (hopefully).

### About This Blog 🤔

Somewhere behind the telescreen there is an alcove. This is mine -- a place for notes on code, on molecules, on books that insist 2 + 2 = 4. **Nothing here is doubleplusgood.**
