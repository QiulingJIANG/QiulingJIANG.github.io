## A few reflections

Since leaving the research institute and starting my job at a university, I feel like my mindset has changed quite a bit. The salary isn't great, the students aren't necessarily the strongest academically, and preparing for classes can be pretty exhausting. But somehow, spending time with students, learning and exchanging ideas with them, has helped ease some of the things that have been weighing on my mind.

At least for a little while, I feel a bit more at ease.
