## Notes on Animal Farm
 Boxer is the strongest animal on the farm and the most loyal. Whenever things go wrong, he says "I will work harder." He gives everything to the windmill, and when he finally collapses, the pigs sell him to the knacker and use the money to buy whisky. The other animals are told he died in a hospital, and most of them believe it. The farm doesn't stop after he's gone. The work goes on, someone else does his share, and after a while nobody really talks about him.  He wasn't punished for anything. He just stopped being useful.

 You're kept while you're useful, and when you're not, you get replaced. There's usually a nice explanation for why. Nobody has to be cruel for this to happen. The system just doesn't need to remember you. Boxer really thought it would pay off. Work hard, stay loyal, things will be fine. I kind of think that way too, if I'm honest. Not saying I'll stop working hard. I just want to be a bit clearer about who it's for, and not hand the whole of my life over to it.
